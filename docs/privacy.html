Map My Friends Privacy Policy

Rosedale Co · Effective October 22, 2026

Map My Friends (“the app”) is published by Rosedale Co (“we”, “us”). The app plots the addresses in your contacts on a map. We built it so your contacts stay on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to that data.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, or receive any of your data on our servers. The app has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Contact addresses are sent to Apple Maps only to look up their map location.
  • Everything else stays on your device.

Contacts

With your permission, the app reads the names, postal addresses, phone numbers, and thumbnail photos of your contacts. It uses this information only to show pins on the map and to let you open, call, or message a contact. Contact information is not copied off your device, except as described under “Apple Maps” below.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your current location to show where you are on the map and to load nearby contacts first. Your location is not stored or sent anywhere by the app.

Apple Maps

To place a contact on the map, the app sends that contact’s postal address (without their name or other details) to Apple’s geocoding service through Apple’s MapKit framework. Apple processes this request under the Apple Privacy Policy. We never receive these requests or their results.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a cache on your device of each contact address and the map coordinates Apple Maps found for it, so later launches are faster. The cache is protected with iOS Data Protection and is not included in device backups. An address is removed from the cache when it is no longer in your contacts, and locations are looked up again after 90 days. Your choices in the app’s Settings are also stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all of this data.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone, because we never have it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Open source

The app’s source code is public on GitHub, so you can check how it handles data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on GitHub.
