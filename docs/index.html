Map My Friends

by Rosedale Co

See your contacts on a map. Map My Friends plots the addresses in your contacts as pins, so you can see who lives near you or near where you’re traveling.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

Some contacts aren’t on the map. Only contacts with a postal address can be shown. If Apple Maps can’t find an address, the app tries again the next time you open it.

How do I change what happens when I tap a pin? Open the iOS Settings app, go to Apps → Map My Friends, and turn “Open Contact on Tap” on or off.

I denied access to Contacts or Location. You can turn access back on in the iOS Settings app under Apps → Map My Friends.

Privacy

Your contacts stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
